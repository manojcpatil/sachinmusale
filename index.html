Commissions

Have your portrait painted

A portrait makes an unusually personal gift — a wedding, a milestone birthday, or remembering someone. Send a clear photograph and I’ll tell you what is possible.

1. Send your photograph

One clear, well-lit photo is enough to start. WhatsApp it straight to me.

2. Choose size and medium

Graphite, charcoal or watercolour; from A4 studies to large framed pieces.

3. Approve and receive

You approve the artwork before it ships. Framing Sold on request.
