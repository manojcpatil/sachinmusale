# Backlink strategy — Sachin Musale

For a regional Indian artist with a Guinness World Record, a 20-year exhibition
record and a teaching academy. The assets are unusually good; almost none of them
are currently working as links.

**Ground rule:** do not buy links, do not use directory-submission services, and do
not swap links with other artists' sites in bulk. A working artist's honest
footprint — press, galleries, institutions, alumni pages — is worth more than a
hundred paid placements and carries no penalty risk. Everything below is real.

**Order matters.** Nothing here works until `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` step 2 is done —
links to `sachinmusale.com` currently land on the old WordPress site.

---

## Tier 1 — do these first (weeks 1–4)

These are near-certain wins, and most are citations you already deserve.

**1. The Guinness World Records entry.** This is the single strongest asset on the
site and it is currently one uncaptioned photograph with a to-do note next to it.
Write it up properly on `exhibitions.html`: what the record was, the date, the
venue, the artist's exact role, who else was involved. Then:
- Link to the official `guinnessworldrecords.com` record page from that write-up.
- Search for the original news coverage of the attempt — Marathi and English —
  and ask those outlets to add a link to the page now documenting it.
A record holder with a documented page is a story local press will re-run.

**2. Google Business Profile** (`business.google.com`). Not a backlink, but the
highest-ROI listing for "artist in Jalgaon" / "drawing classes Jalgaon". Name,
address, phone and hours must match `contact.html` exactly.

**3. Local press, in Marathi.** Jalgaon and Khandesh outlets have covered the
Rangotsav exhibition (~6,000 visitors over eight days) and the academy before.
Target: *Lokmat* (Jalgaon edition), *Sakal*, *Deshonnati*, *Divya Marathi*,
*Tarun Bharat*, plus Jalgaon-district news portals. Pitch the record and the
1,000 Faces project, not the website. Ask for a link to
`sachinmusale.com/1000-faces.html` in the piece.

**4. Exhibition venues and organisers.** Juhu Art Gallery (Mumbai), the Aurangabad
and Jalgaon venues, Vava Vachanalaya Multipurpose Hall, the Rangotsav organisers.
Galleries keep artist pages and past-exhibition archives. Ask each to link the
artist name to `sachinmusale.com/about.html`.

**5. Art-college and alumni pages.** The Master in Fine Arts institution, and any
school where the academy's students sat the Maharashtra elementary/intermediate
drawing exams. `.edu`/`.ac.in` alumni and faculty pages are high-trust and
routinely added on request.

## Tier 2 — artist platforms (weeks 3–8)

Each is a real profile, filled in properly, linking back. Do not spray these —
five complete profiles beat thirty empty ones.

| Platform | Why |
|---|---|
| **Instagram + Facebook** | Already linked in schema `sameAs`. Put the site URL in the bio; it is currently the only path from the largest audience. |
| **Behance / ArtStation** | Strong domains, index well for artist names. |
| **Saatchi Art, Artmajeur, Artfinder** | Marketplaces that accept an artist website link. Worth it even if you never sell through them. |
| **Wikidata** | Free, permanent, and feeds Google's knowledge panel. A Guinness record holder with press coverage plausibly meets the bar. Add `official website` = the site. |
| **Artist-run YouTube** | The five process videos already on `exhibitions.html` are on YouTube. Put the site in every description and in the channel's link section. |

## Tier 3 — content that earns links (months 2–6)

The site currently has no page anyone links to *for information*. That is the gap.
Each of these is a page that other people cite:

**"How the Maharashtra elementary and intermediate drawing examinations work."**
Syllabus, grades, what's assessed, how to prepare, timeline. Parents and teachers
across Maharashtra search this and there is little good English/Marathi material.
This is the best link magnet available and it sits squarely in the academy's
expertise. Link it from `classes.html`.

**"Watercolour on location in Khandesh."** The 18 landscapes were painted on site —
ghats, temples, boatyards, Jal Mahal. A page pairing each painting with the place
attracts links from travel, heritage and regional-culture sites, and picks up
place-name searches the artwork pages cannot.

**The 1,000 Faces project as an ongoing story.** A numbered, never-repeated portrait
series is inherently newsworthy and re-coverable — at 100, at 250, at 500. Update
the counter (it currently reads 0) and give the press a reason to come back.

## What to skip

- Paid guest posts, link packages, PBNs, "1,000 directory submissions".
- Comment and forum-signature links.
- Mass reciprocal link exchanges with other artist sites.
- Fiverr/Upwork "SEO backlink" gigs. These build exactly the pattern Google's
  link-spam systems were built to discount.

## Measuring it

Search Console → **Links** shows Google's own view; that is the number that counts.
Check monthly, not weekly. Realistic expectation for a regional artist: **15–30
genuine referring domains in the first year**, most of them press, venues and
institutions. That is more than enough to rank for "Sachin Musale", "artist
Jalgaon", "watercolour portrait commission India" and the exam-preparation terms.
